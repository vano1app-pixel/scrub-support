Scrub — Privacy

Data collected: none.

Scrub works entirely on your iPhone. It does not have accounts, analytics, tracking, advertising or a server. Files and recordings never leave the device unless you share them yourself with the share sheet.

Purchases

Paid features are sold by Apple as an in-app purchase or subscription. Apple handles the payment and the receipt — Scrub only reads whether your purchase is valid. You can restore purchases in the app and manage or cancel subscriptions in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Permissions

Scrub asks for no permissions. It reads only the photos you pick and hands clean copies back to you.
